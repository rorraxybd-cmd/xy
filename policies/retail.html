Official Guidelines

Retail Customer Policy

Ensuring absolute transparency, 24/7 dedicated service, and premium shopping standards across rorraxy.

🛒 1. Order Placement & Digital Authentication

  • Order Confirmation: Orders are officially logged and confirmed instantly once digital payment or verification checkpoints clear successfully.
  • Real-Time Dashboard: Buyers receive automated tracking references and live status updates directly through their account portal.
  • Seamless UX: Checkout and cart additions operate smoothly without disruptive notification popups, maintaining a clean user interface.

✨ 2. Product Authenticity & Quality

  • Original Inventory: Every product cataloged in the retail ecosystem undergoes strict quality assurance to guarantee 100% authenticity.
  • Accurate Descriptions: Specifications, features, and imagery are maintained accurately to reflect the true nature of delivered goods.

💰 3. Transparent Pricing & Multi-Tier Discounts

  • Clear Costing: Listed prices include all standard applicable local taxes and base handling costs with no hidden fees at checkout.
  • Tiered Savings: Eligible promotional discounts ranging from minor tier reductions to bulk perks are applied automatically based on active ecosystem rules.

🌍 4. Global Fulfillment & Custom Crafting

  • Cross-Border Logistics: Orders involving cross-border transit or international dispatch are processed securely through established global logistics networks.
  • Customized Production: For bespoke or specialized custom products, extended crafting and handling schedules apply transparently prior to final dispatch.
  • Secure Transit: All packages feature tamper-evident sealing to guarantee absolute product integrity upon arrival.

⚡ 5. 24/7 Customer Assistance & Service

  • Round-the-Clock Availability: Our support infrastructure operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week to handle inquiries, tracking assistance, and general guidance.
  • Rapid Response SLA: Dedicated client success desks ensure timely communications across all active ecosystem touchpoints.

🛡️ 6. Buyer Protection & Replacements

  • Instant Cancellation: Unfulfilled orders can be cancelled directly via the user dashboard before logistics handover.
  • Damage Claims: In case of transit defects or wrong items, customers can file a secure support ticket within 48 hours for expedited resolution.
