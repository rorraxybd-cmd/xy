Merchant Operational Framework

Seller Merchant Terms

Unified onboarding protocols, inventory compliance, settlement schedules, and merchant governance across rorraxy seller networks.

📝 1. Unified Merchant Authentication & KYC

  • Ecosystem Network Access: Merchants operating across `v.rorraxy.com`, `seller.rorraxy.com`, and `vendor.rorraxy.com` must complete unified business verification, submitting official tax registration, trade credentials, and corporate bank details.
  • Merchant Store Routing: Approved vendors are assigned verified store routing protocols via `s.rorraxy.com`, ensuring secure storefront redirection and catalog integrity.
  • Account Integrity: Sellers are strictly responsible for preserving active verification credentials, authorized payout nodes, and dashboard security settings.

🏷️ 2. Catalog Integrity & Anti-Counterfeit Standards

  • Zero Counterfeit Tolerance: Merchants must exclusively list 100% original, authentic, and authorized goods. Publishing counterfeit, prohibited, or unauthorized stock triggers immediate ecosystem suspension.
  • Accurate Product Data: Specifications, inventory allocations, and pricing structures across storefront portals must reflect true, real-time product conditions.

📦 3. Order Processing & Dispatch Timelines

  • Strict Fulfillment Timelines: Sellers are required to process, package, and hand over order consignments to authorized logistics channels within 24 to 48 business hours of order placement.
  • Tamper-Evident Packaging: Merchants must employ tamper-evident protective sealing to preserve merchandise condition during national and cross-border transit.

💳 4. Settlement Schedules & Marketplace Fees

  • Automated Settlement Cycles: Verified net seller revenues are disbursed to registered merchant bank accounts or global payout gateways following standard order delivery confirmation windows.
  • Transparent Ledger Itemization: Category commission structures, processing fees, and handling adjustments are automatically itemized within the merchant financial dashboard.

⚡ 5. 24/7 Enterprise Seller Support Desk

  • Continuous Merchant Support: Sellers maintain 24/7 access to specialized operational desks for catalog management, logistics routing, and settlement verification.
  • Expedited Issue Resolution: Dedicated account managers prioritize merchant inquiries to ensure uninterrupted commercial workflows.

🛡️ 6. Platform Governance & Dispute Audits

  • Operational Performance Metrics: Stores exhibiting elevated order cancellation rates, dispatch delays, or quality disputes undergo compliance reviews and tier adjustments.
  • Equitable Claim Assessment: Buyer returns or defect claims are evaluated objectively using verified logistics logs and merchant fulfillment documentation.
