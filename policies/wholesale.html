B2B & Commercial Policy

Wholesale & B2B Terms

International bulk procurement standards, advance settlement framework, and global B2B logistics guidelines across rorraxy.

🧪 1. Paid Sample Order & Evaluation

  • Mandatory Sample Protocol: Prior to committing to large commercial volumes, B2B buyers must initiate a paid sample order via `b2b.rorraxy.com` to evaluate build quality and product specifications.
  • 100% Upfront Sample Payment: Sample requests, custom prototypes, and initial shipping handling charges must be fully prepaid through active card payments or integrated gateway solutions.

🏦 2. Bulk Order Settlement & Wire Transfer (T/T)

  • Pre-Production Advance Settlement: All confirmed bulk commercial orders operate on a strict 100% advance payment settlement schedule prior to factory dispatch or custom mass production.
  • Verified Direct Bank Transfer: Institutional bulk payments must be settled via international Wire Transfer / Telegraphic Transfer (T/T), Letter of Credit (L/C), or approved corporate merchant gateways.
  • Official Proforma Invoicing: Order manufacturing commences immediately upon formal bank deposit confirmation matching the issued Proforma Invoice (PI).

📦 3. Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) & Scale Pricing

  • Strict MOQ Thresholds: Wholesale pricing tiers are systematically tied to category-specific Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ).
  • Dynamic Volume Discounts: Larger commercial allocations unlock optimized unit pricing structures, automatically calculated on official price quotes.

🌍 4. Cross-Border Freight & Custom Crafting

  • Pre-Dispatched Clearance: Shipments are handed over to accredited international freight forwarders (Air/Sea) only after full payment settlement is confirmed.
  • Customization Timelines: Bespoke manufacturing or branded bulk runs require dedicated production windows, explicitly agreed upon in the commercial quotation.
  • Export Documentation: Complete commercial invoices, packing lists, and origin declarations are provided digitally via the partner dashboard.

⚡ 5. 24/7 Enterprise Key Account Assistance

  • Round-the-Clock Priority Desk: Institutional B2B clients receive 24/7 direct communication support for freight tracking, invoice clearance, and order status checks.
  • SLA Commitment: Dedicated account managers handle commercial inquiries with immediate operational priority.

🛡️ 6. Bulk Consignment Audit & Claims

  • 7-Day Audit Window: Buyers must inspect delivered commercial pallets and notify `rorraxy` of any verified manufacturing defects within 7 business days.
  • Commercial Resolution: Valid transit or manufacturing claims are rectified through authorized replacement shipments or commercial credit memos.
