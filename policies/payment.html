Financial Security & Compliance

Payment, Security & Refund Policy

Secure global transaction protocols, advance settlement frameworks, and transparent refund standards across rorraxy ecosystems.

💳 1. Secure Payment Methods & Gateways

  • Multi-Channel Transactions: Payments across retail, wholesale, and solutions portals are processed through verified International Payment Gateways (IPG), secure card networks, and localized mobile financial services.
  • Corporate Wire Settlements: Institutional wholesale and business solution milestones support direct bank wire transfers (T/T), Telegraphic Transfers, and Letter of Credit (L/C) verifications.

🔒 2. Transaction Security & Encryption

  • Advanced Encryption Standards: All financial data and transaction communications are secured using robust end-to-end encryption protocols, ensuring absolute user confidentiality.
  • Zero Sensitive Storage: Financial credentials and sensitive card details are never stored directly on ecosystem servers, mitigating unauthorized data access risks.

📑 3. Advance Settlement Policy (Wholesale & Solutions)

  • Wholesale Advance Terms: Bulk B2B orders require 100% advance settlement via verified bank transfers prior to factory processing and international freight dispatch.
  • Solutions Milestone Framework: Business solutions operate on a strict 50% advance retainer and 50% final handover settlement structure as defined in formal service contracts.

🔄 4. Refund, Cancellation & Dispute Framework

  • Retail Cancellation: Unfulfilled retail orders can be cancelled directly via user dashboards prior to warehouse dispatch for immediate refund processing.
  • Service & Customization Non-Refundability: Due to allocated architectural resources, milestone retainers for business solutions and custom wholesale sample orders are strictly non-refundable once production or development commences.

⚡ 5. 24/7 Financial Support Assistance

  • Round-the-Clock Reconciliation: Dedicated financial support desks operate 24/7 to resolve gateway verification queries, invoice settlements, and refund tracking status.
  • Transparent Dispute Audits: Transaction anomalies or claim discrepancies are audited fairly against secure gateway logs and banking records.

⚖️ 6. Regulatory Compliance & Updates

  • Global Financial Standards: Payment frameworks adhere strictly to international merchant processing guidelines and regional compliance mandates.
  • Policy Revisions: Financial terms are subject to periodic updates to reflect enhanced security standards across all active ecosystem portals.
